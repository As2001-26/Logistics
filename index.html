FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers!

Find clear answers to the most common questions about our logistics services.

What logistics services do you offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of logistics services including air freight, ocean freight, road transportation, warehousing, and customs clearance tailored to your business needs.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times vary depending on the chosen shipping method and destination. Domestic road freight usually takes 1-3 days, while international air freight can take 3-5 days.

Do you provide international shipping?

Yes, we have a vast global network and provide reliable international shipping to over 150 countries worldwide, managing all complex documentation.

How can I track my shipment?

You can track your shipment 24/7 using our online tracking portal by simply entering your unique tracking number provided at booking.

What if my cargo is delayed or damaged?

We provide comprehensive cargo insurance options. In the rare event of damage or delay, our dedicated support team will assist you immediately with the claims process.

How do I get a quote for my shipment?

You can easily get a custom quote by filling out the 'Request Quote' form on our website or by directly contacting one of our logistics experts.
